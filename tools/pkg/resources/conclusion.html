HomePad is installed

HomePad is in your Applications folder. Click Close: HomePad then opens by itself. Follow its welcome window to give it the three permissions you just read about.

Using it

Press Option-Space to open HomePad and press it again to close it, or click the grid icon in the menu bar at the top of the screen. Right-click that icon for Settings and to quit.

If HomePad didn't open

Wait a few seconds after closing this window. If it still isn't there, open Applications in Finder and double-click HomePad.

If your Mac can't move the installer to the Trash

Click OK. The installer still has the disk image open at that moment; it ejects it right after. Then delete HomePad-Installer.dmg from Downloads.
