HomePad is installed

HomePad is in your Applications folder, and it is opening now. Follow its welcome window to give it the three permissions you just read about.

Using it

Press Option-Space to open HomePad and press it again to close it, or click the grid icon in the menu bar at the top of the screen. Right-click that icon for Settings and to quit.

If HomePad didn't open

Open Applications in Finder and double-click HomePad.

If your Mac can't move the installer to the Trash

That happens when an earlier HomePad-Installer.dmg is already in the Trash. It's harmless: click OK, then delete HomePad-Installer.dmg from Downloads.
