What to expect

After installing, HomePad's welcome window asks for three permissions. Each one is optional, but HomePad works best with all three. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Screen Recording: previews of your windows

1Click the red Allow… button. A message with a padlock appears.
2Click Open System Settings in that message.
3Turn on the switch next to HomePad, then click Quit & Reopen. HomePad restarts by itself.

Accessibility: jump to a window, and the double-tap shortcut

1Click Allow…, then Open System Settings in the padlock message.
2Turn on the switch next to HomePad. The welcome window comes back by itself.

Full Disk Access: search in Documents, Desktop and Downloads

1Click Allow…. System Settings opens, with a small HomePad window beside it.
2Turn on the switch next to HomePad. If it isn't in the list, drag the icon from the small window into the list first. Then click Quit & Reopen.

Then choose how to open HomePad (the standard shortcut is Option-Space) and click Get started.
