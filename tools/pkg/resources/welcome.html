Welcome to HomePad

HomePad is a launcher for your Mac: press a shortcut and a panel opens with a search bar and your apps, in categories you choose. It can replace the Dock.

This installer puts HomePad in your Applications folder and opens it. It takes less than a minute.

What happens next

  1. Click Continue through the next screens, then Install. Your Mac asks for your password once.
  2. When you close the installer, HomePad opens by itself and shows its own welcome window, which asks for three permissions. The next screen explains them.

Needs macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer. Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
